## 🎒 Essential Gear

### Group Equipment
- Tents with footprints
- Stove, fuel, cookware
- Water filter / purification tablets
- Multitool and repair kit
- First-aid kit
- Bear-proof food storage

### Personal Equipment
- Backpack (30–35 lbs load)
- Sleeping bag and pad
- Layered clothing system
- Headlamp with spare batteries
- Map, compass, GPS (optional)
- 3+ quarts water capacity

> 💡 *Conduct a test pack at home to check weight distribution and identify missing items.*

---

## 🛡️ Safety & Emergency Protocols

- Share detailed itinerary with a trusted contact.
- Carry satellite communicator in remote areas.
- Store food in bear canisters or hang 10–12 ft above ground.
- Establish clear decision triggers for weather or fatigue.
- First-aid kit must include blister care, antiseptics, and pain relievers.
- Know nearest ranger station and emergency contacts.

## 🌿 Leave No Trace Commitment

1. Plan ahead and know regulations.
2. Travel and camp on durable surfaces.
3. Pack out all trash and food scraps.
4. Leave natural objects undisturbed.
5. Minimize campfire impact (use stoves where possible).
6. Respect wildlife—observe from a distance.
7. Be considerate of other visitors.
